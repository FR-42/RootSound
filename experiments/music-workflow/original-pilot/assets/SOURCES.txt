The dataset's original readme identifies f1 and f3 as soprano and f8 as
mezzo-soprano. Their identities are anonymous. They are recordings of
individual female singers sustaining the vowel a, not text-to-speech.
Each original WAV includes multiple long notes separated by pauses.

The downloaded WAVs are unmodified. Derived instruments/audio in the pilot
may trim, tune, loop, change pitch/duration, add effects and mix these
recordings. Such processing is a modification and does not imply endorsement
by the dataset creators or performers. Keep this attribution and the license
with any distributed project or derived sample instrument using these sounds.

vocalset-pitch-windows.json contains signal-estimated windows and frequencies.
These are production aids, not a listening verdict or proof of naturalness.
